# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone interacting in this project's issues, PRs and discussions —
maintainers, contributors, and AI agents acting on someone's behalf — is
expected to keep the space technical, kind and free of harassment.

## The short version

- Review code, not people.
- Assume competence and good intent; ask before ascribing either fault or folly.
- No harassment, discriminatory language, or unrequested self-promotion.
- Disagree about designs with evidence; take personality clashes off-thread.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers via a private GitHub message to
`@context-saver/maintainers`. Maintainers may remove comments and block
participants who repeatedly violate this conduct after one warning.

This is a deliberately short, plain-language conduct standard. It is
inspired by the Contributor Covenant but written to be readable in one
minute.
